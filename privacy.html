Privacy policy

Effective date: 22 November 2026

This policy explains what information the game UBall27 (the "Game", Android package com.openapex.uball27) collects, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. The Game is published by Open Apex Int. ("we", "us"). If you have any question, write to apex.open.int@gmail.com.

1. Summary

  • You can play without creating an account. Your careers are saved on your device.
  • If you are online, the Game signs you in to Unity Gaming Services with an anonymous player ID so it can back up your careers and show leaderboards.
  • You can choose to create a username and password account to restore your careers on another device.
  • The Game shows ads through Unity LevelPlay. Ads are only personalised if you allow it.
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play. We never see your payment card or bank details.
  • You can ask us to delete your account and data at any time. See Delete your account.

2. Information we collect

The player name you type when you create a career is the name of your fictional footballer. Please do not use your real full name or other personal details as your player name or username, because leaderboard names are public.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data.

3. Ads and your choices

The Game is free and is supported by ads. Ads are provided through Unity LevelPlay, a service of Unity Technologies, and the ad networks it connects to. These partners may use your device's advertising ID and similar information to show ads, count views and prevent fraud.

  • Personalised ads: the Game asks for your choice and only shows personalised ads if you agree. You can change this at any time in the Game's Settings under "Personalised ads".
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings under Google › Ads.
  • Rewarded ads are always optional: you choose when to watch them.

You can read how Unity handles data in the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

4. Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play under the Google Privacy Policy. We receive only the information needed to deliver your purchase, such as the product and an order reference. We never receive your card number or bank details.

5. Who we share information with

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with the service providers that run parts of the Game for us:

  • Unity Technologies: Unity Gaming Services (Authentication, Cloud Save, Leaderboards), Unity Cloud Diagnostics and Unity LevelPlay ad mediation.
  • Ad networks connected through LevelPlay, such as Unity Ads.
  • Google: Google Play, for downloads and purchases.

These providers may process data outside your country, including in the United States, under their own safeguards. We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

6. How long we keep information

  • Saves on your device stay until you delete them in the Game or uninstall the Game.
  • Cloud backups, accounts and leaderboard entries are kept while you use the Game, and are deleted when you ask us to delete your account.
  • Crash reports and advertising data are kept by Unity and the ad networks according to their own policies.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, including in the European Union and the United Kingdom under the GDPR, you have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal data, to object to or restrict its use, and to withdraw consent at any time. To use these rights, email apex.open.int@gmail.com and tell us your in-game username or describe your account. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

8. Children

The Game is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

Information sent to Unity and Google is protected with encrypted connections. No system is completely secure, so please use a password for your account that you do not use anywhere else.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the Game changes. We will change the effective date at the top of this page and, for important changes, tell you in the Game.
