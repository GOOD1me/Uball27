Account deletion

Delete your UBall27 account and data

This page explains how to delete the account and data of the game UBall27 by Open Apex Int. You do not need to have the game installed.

How to ask for deletion

  1. Send an email to apex.open.int@gmail.com with the subject UBall27 account deletion.
  2. Include your UBall27 username. If you never created a username, write the name of your footballer and your club as shown in the game, so we can find your data.
  3. Send the email from an address you can reply from, in case we need to confirm the request is yours.

We delete your data within 30 days and reply to confirm when it is done.

What we delete

  • Your UBall27 account, including your username and sign-in details.
  • Your player ID.
  • Your career backups stored in the cloud, including the purchase records kept with them.
  • Your scores and names on the online leaderboards.

What is not deleted by us

  • Saves on your phone: they are stored only on your device. Delete them in the game, or uninstall the game, to remove them.
  • Google Play purchase history: Google keeps its own order records under the Google Privacy Policy. After deletion, purchases can no longer be restored to your deleted UBall27 account.
  • Advertising data: ad partners keep data under their own policies. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings under Google › Ads.
  • Records we must keep by law: for example, records of a deletion request, for as long as the law requires.

Questions about your data? Read our privacy policy or write to apex.open.int@gmail.com.
